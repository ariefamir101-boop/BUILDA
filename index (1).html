How to play

Every correct answer powers your blaster.

🎯

1. Aim at an answer

Read the question above the arena. Click or tap the moving target that shows the answer you believe is correct.

⚡

2. Power up

A correct answer earns points and energy. Your blaster uses that power to tag a rival bot. Wrong answers cost a little energy—try again!

🏆

3. Win the round

Answer all 10 questions before time runs out. Build a streak for bonus points and see your final score and learning feedback.
